Create your own reading corner

A comfortable chair, thoughtful lighting, and a few familiar details can turn an unused corner into a favourite spot.

By Vaidehi Craft

In this story

  1. Choose your corner first
  2. Think beyond appearance
  3. Layer the lighting
  4. Keep the details useful
← All stories

01

Choose your corner first

Look for a part of the room where you can settle without blocking a busy walkway. A place near daylight can feel inviting, but consider glare at the times you usually read.

Measure the available area before choosing a chair. Include space for a side table and lamp, and check that nearby doors and drawers will still open comfortably.

02

Think beyond appearance

Seat height, depth, back support, and arm position all affect how a chair feels. Think about whether you prefer to sit upright with a book or curl up with a cushion.

If possible, try the chair before choosing. Ask the team about dimensions and cushion options so you can compare the design with a seat you already find comfortable.

03

Layer the lighting

Daylight and a reading lamp serve different purposes. Position a lamp so you can see the page clearly without an exposed bulb shining directly into your eyes.

Choose the lamp alongside the chair and table rather than as an afterthought. Check its height and the route of its cable to keep the corner easy to use.

04

Keep the details useful

A small table gives a cup, glasses, and your current book a home. A throw or cushion adds a personal touch without filling the corner with extra furniture.

Leave a little empty space around the arrangement. The aim is a corner that invites you to pause, with everything you need close by.
